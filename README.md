# 🎯 Main Functionalities

## ➕ Addition

Adds two numbers entered by the user.

---

## ➖ Subtraction

Subtracts the second number from the first number.

---

## ✖ Multiplication

Multiplies two numbers.

---

## ➗ Division

Divides two numbers with divide-by-zero protection.

---

## 🔢 Power

Calculates the power of a number.

Example:

```python
2 ^ 3 = 8
```

---

## 🧮 Modulus

Finds the remainder after division.

Example:

```python
10 % 3 = 1
```

---

## 📝 Calculation History

Stores all calculations performed during execution.

Example:

```python
5 + 5 = 10
9 × 3 = 27
10 ÷ 2 = 5
```

---
